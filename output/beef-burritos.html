Beef Burritos

Published: Thu 20 August 2026
By Anthony

In Cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ground beef
  • 2 onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 2 tsp oregano
  • Half a tube tomato purée
  • 1 tin kidney beans
  • 1 tin of tomatoes
  • 1 tin of water
  • Salt/pepper

Fillings: rice, cheese, salsa/tomato, lettuce, sour cream, avocado/guac, lime/coriander.

Method

Fry the onions in a tablespoon of coconut oil until slightly browned.

Hard fry the beef in a heavy based pan until fully browned.

Drain off any excess fat.

Add the rest of the ingredients taking care to not burn the spices.

Cook for an hour.
